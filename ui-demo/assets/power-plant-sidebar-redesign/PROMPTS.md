# Power Plant Sidebar Redesign — 素材生成提示词

以下两次生成均使用两张项目指定参考图作为“材质与压迫感参考”，不复制其构图、人物、文字或可识别设计。正式 Demo 不使用参考图本身，只使用经过确定性后处理的原创材质贴图。

## 湿蚀生物工业钢材

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless material tile for an offline 280px Electron Canvas prototype
Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 are mood/material references only; do not reproduce any pod layout, characters, text, composition, or identifiable artwork.
Primary request: create an original square tile of wet, corroded bio-industrial steel from a gigantic underground human power plant.
Style/medium: dense comic ink texture fused with realistic worn metal; restrained detail that survives downsampling to 256×256.
Composition/framing: straight-on orthographic material sample, edge-to-edge, approximately seamless, no single focal object.
Lighting/mood: dim cold green-black ambient light with tiny oxidized highlights; oppressive and damp.
Color palette: charcoal, green-black, muted gunmetal, traces of old rust; no bright neon.
Materials/textures: brushed steel, grease, condensation streaks, etched maintenance scratches, shallow rivet scars, fine cross-hatched ink grain.
Constraints: material only; no machinery silhouette, pods, people, labels, text, logos, borders, frames, scene depth, watermark, or copyrighted composition.
```

## 暗红培养液与污玻璃

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless fluid-and-glass material tile for an offline 280px Electron Canvas prototype
Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 are mood and material references only; do not reproduce their pods, people, text, composition, or identifiable artwork.

Style/medium: gritty graphic-novel ink mixed with restrained realistic liquid texture; detail must survive downsampling to 256×256.
Composition/framing: straight-on orthographic material sample, edge-to-edge, approximately seamless, no focal object and no complete human figure.

Color palette: oxblood, dried crimson, black-red, tiny pale mineral highlights; no green or cyan.
Materials/textures: viscous fluid currents, small suspended particles, fogged glass smears, thin capillary silhouettes, cross-hatched ink.
Constraints: material only; no pod frame, no machinery silhouette, no readable body, labels, text, logos, borders, UI, scene depth, watermark, or copyrighted composition.
```
